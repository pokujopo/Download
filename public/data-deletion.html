Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: 29 October 2026

This page explains how to revoke a social-platform connection and request deletion of information associated with your ZynkPost account.

1. Revoke a social-platform connection

Open the relevant social platform’s account settings and remove ZynkPost from the list of apps or services you have authorized. The platform’s menus may change over time. Revoking access prevents future API actions that require that authorization, but it does not by itself delete content already published to that platform.

2. Request deletion from ZynkPost

Email pokujopo@gmail.com from an address you can access. Use the subject “ZynkPost data deletion request” and tell us the account identifier or social connection you want deleted. Do not email passwords or access tokens.

We may ask for reasonable information to verify your request. We will review and process valid deletion requests in accordance with applicable law and any necessary security or legal-retention requirements.

3. What happens after a request

We will remove or de-identify information covered by an approved request where deletion is appropriate and technically possible. Information needed to comply with law, prevent fraud, resolve disputes or maintain security may be retained for the period required for those purposes. Removing data from ZynkPost does not delete posts already published on a third-party platform; manage those posts directly on that platform.
